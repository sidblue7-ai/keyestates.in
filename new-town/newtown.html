New Town

New Town, located on the northeastern edge of Kolkata, is a meticulously planned area under the New Town Kolkata Development Authority, within the North 24 Parganas district. This dynamic locale features a mix of residential developments, commercial and retail complexes, corporate parks, and institutional entities.

New Town is rapidly emerging as a significant employment hub in Kolkata, alongside Salt Lake Sector V. Its strategic development includes educational institutions, retail spaces, and top-tier hospitals.

A key highlight is its planned layout integrating residential, commercial, IT, and institutional zones. The area boasts excellent road connectivity and upcoming metro expansion.

In summary, New Town offers modern living with strong infrastructure, making it a prime destination for both homebuyers and investors.
