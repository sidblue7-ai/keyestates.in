PS Amistad- Project Overview

PS Amistad: Premium Living in Action Area II, New Town

PS Amistad is a thoughtfully designed residential project situated in the rapidly growing area of Action Area II, New Town. Developed by the renowned PS Group, this premium project offers a modern, comfortable, and well-connected lifestyle for its residents. With a strategic location, world-class amenities, and luxurious apartments, PS Amistad stands out as an ideal choice for homebuyers looking for convenience and upscale living in Kolkata.

Spread across 1 acre of prime land, PS One 10 is thoughtfully planned with only 55 exclusive residences, to maximise natural light, ventilation, and panoramic views—perfect for modern families seeking comfort with elegance. PS One 10 stands apart from other real estate projects with its limited-edition, low-density living concept —offering spacious, lake-facing luxury homes in the heart of an IT-driven micro-market, where such exclusivity is increasingly rare.

Location Advantage of PS Amistad

Located in the heart of Action Area II, New Town, Amistad benefits from the region’s excellent infrastructure and seamless connectivity. New Town is one of Kolkata’s most rapidly developing areas, known for its thriving residential and commercial growth. With Salt Lake, Rajarhat, and the Central Business District within easy reach, residents of Amistad enjoy quick access to key parts of the city. Major roads like the Major Arterial Road (MAR) and the New Town Expressway provide seamless connectivity to the rest of Kolkata, making commuting convenient. Public transportation options, including buses, taxis, and app-based services, make daily travel easy. Additionally, the upcoming New Garia-Airport Metro Line will further enhance connectivity across the city. For frequent travellers, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport is just 20 minutes away, adding to the convenience of living at Amistad.

A Proximity to Key Hubs and Amenities

Living in PS Amistad means being close to Kolkata’s major IT hubs, top educational institutions, and healthcare centres. The region is surrounded by IT parks like Ecospace and DLF IT Park, making it a prime location for professionals working in the IT and corporate sectors. Leading educational institutions like Amity University are nearby, offering quality education opportunities for families. The area also features several shopping malls such as Axis Mall and City Centre 2, providing residents with numerous options for shopping, dining, and entertainment. Healthcare facilities like Tata Medical Center and Ohio Hospital ensure that medical care is always within reach. Amistad provides a well-rounded lifestyle, balancing modern conveniences with a thriving urban environment.

PS Amistad: A Well-Designed Living Space

Spread over 1.08 acres of land, PS Amistad comprises 242 residential units, offering an ideal environment for modern living. The project includes three towers with configurations of G+15, B+G+17, and B+G+18 floors, ensuring spacious living with ample open areas. The apartments feature 3 BHK units designed with comfort, privacy, and luxury in mind. The homes at Amistad are built to optimise natural light and ventilation, promoting a healthy and pleasant living experience. With a super built-up area ranging from 712 sq. ft. to 969 sq. ft. the project caters to various family sizes and preferences.

A Lifestyle of Convenience and Luxury

PS Amistad offers a wide range of lifestyle amenities for residents, including a fully equipped gym, swimming pool, and indoor game areas. The project is designed to foster community living with a community hall and multipurpose courts, allowing residents to enjoy leisure activities and socialize. With round-the-clock security systems, including 24/7 surveillance and manned personnel, Amistad ensures the safety and peace of mind of its residents. Additionally, the project incorporates eco-friendly features like rainwater harvesting, contributing to sustainability and a greener future.

Conclusion

Amistad by PS Group is the epitome of modern, premium living in Action Area II, New Town. Offering excellent connectivity, luxurious apartments, and top-tier amenities, PS Amistad is designed to meet the lifestyle needs of today’s urban families and professionals. Its strategic location near IT hubs, educational institutions, and healthcare facilities, combined with its competitive pricing, makes it a valuable investment in Kolkata’s rapidly evolving real estate landscape. Whether you’re a professional seeking proximity to the city’s business districts or a family looking for a well-connected, luxury home, PS Amistad stands out as the ideal choice for your future.
