Primarc The Soul - Project Overview

Welcome to Primarc The Soul: A Luxurious Residential Experience in New Town, Kolkata

Welcome to Primarc The Soul, an exquisite residential apartment located in the vibrant heart of Action Area III, New Town, Kolkata. Developed by the renowned Primarc Group, Primarc The Soul stands as a testament to modern architecture and thoughtful design, catering to the diverse needs of families and individuals seeking a luxurious living experience.

Prime Location in Action Area III

Action Area III is rapidly emerging as one of Kolkata’s most sought-after residential and commercial districts. Its strategic location near the bustling Biswa Bangla Sarani offers residents unparalleled access to major transport networks, making it a convenient choice for both work and leisure. With seamless connectivity to prominent neighbourhoods such as Salt Lake, Sector V, and Rajarhat, Action Area III is not only an ideal residential 1locale but also a thriving business hub

Primarc The Soul benefits immensely from this prime location, allowing residents to enjoy the vibrant atmosphere of New Town. Essential amenities such as shopping centres, educational institutions, and healthcare facilities are within easy reach, ensuring a well-rounded lifestyle. The project is also situated close to key IT parks, making it perfect for professionals who desire a dynamic work-life balance.

Thoughtful Design and Spacious Living

Spread over an expansive 6.89 acres of land, Primarc The Soul is meticulously designed to provide an idyllic living environment. The residential project comprises 10 blocks with a G+16 floor configuration, featuring a total of 704 residential units. The apartments are thoughtfully designed and range from 3BHK layouts, accommodating families of all sizes. The price range for these well-crafted residences starts at ₹1.30 Cr onwards, offering a range of options for prospective homeowners. The possession date for The Soul is set for September 2025, giving buyers ample time to plan their move into this remarkable community.

Exceptional Amenities and Community Spirit

Primarc The Soul offers a harmonious blend of contemporary residential, recreational, and wellness features. From lush green spaces to thoughtfully designed communal areas, every aspect of The Soul is geared towards fostering a sense of community and belonging. Residents can indulge in various recreational facilities that enhance their lifestyle, making it a perfect choice for families and individuals alike.
