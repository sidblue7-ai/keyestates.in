Action Area III

New Town Action Area III, a burgeoning locality in the northeastern part of Kolkata, is swiftly evolving into a dynamic hub encompassing residential, commercial, and institutional zones. Surrounded by Action Areas I & II, as well as the village areas of Ganragari, Hudarait, Dharmatala Pachuria, Kulberia, and Chandakanthal Beria, this locality is seamlessly connected via the New Town Road, which links it with Bishwa Bangla Sarani Road (NH-34), ensuring extensive connectivity.

With the burgeoning demand for contemporary living spaces, Action Area III is witnessing a surge in residential projects catering to diverse homebuyers. From luxurious high-rise apartments equipped with state-of-the-art amenities to thoughtfully designed affordable housing options, the locality offers a spectrum of choices.

Residents of Action Area III relish a balanced lifestyle, benefiting from easy access to commercial zones, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and recreational spaces. As this vibrant locality continues to thrive, it promises a harmonious blend of urban convenience and modern living for its residents
