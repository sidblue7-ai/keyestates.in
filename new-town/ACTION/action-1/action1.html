Action Area I

New Town Action Area I, is an emerging locality in North Kolkata, that offers a harmonious blend of residential and commercial complexes. Surrounded by New Town Action Area II, Salt Lake Sector V, Kestopur, Baguiati, and Rajarhat, this well-planned locale enjoys decent connectivity via Biswa Bangla Sarani Road (NH-34) and several internal roads. Anticipated to enhance accessibility further, the under-construction Line 6, or Yellow Line (New Garia-Airport Line), is poised to introduce metro connectivity by 2021.

Action Area I boasts an array of essential amenities, including schools, hospitals, shopping complexes, malls, recreational spots, and hotels, fostering a vibrant atmosphere. As a rapidly evolving region, it presents diverse real estate opportunities to investors and homebuyers alike.

Strategically located near major transportation hubs like the Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport, Action Area I holds significant appeal. Its well-planned infrastructure, featuring wide roads and verdant green spaces, contributes to its allure. As part of New Town, residents benefit from access to modern amenities, ensuring a vibrant and livable community ambiance in Action Area I.
