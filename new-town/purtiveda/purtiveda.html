Purti Veda - Project Overview

Purti Veda: Your Gateway to Modern Living in Action Area I, New Town

Located in the heart of Action Area I, New Town, Purti Veda is a thoughtfully designed residential project offering a seamless blend of modern living and a community-centric lifestyle. Developed by the esteemed Purti Realty, this project provides homebuyers with a well-planned residential complex equipped with contemporary amenities, making it an ideal choice for families seeking comfort, convenience, and quality.

Prime Location in Action Area I, New Town

Action Area I, New Town is one of Kolkata’s most sought-after residential and commercial zones. Known for its strategic urban planning and modern infrastructure, this area has become a prime destination for homebuyers and businesses alike. As part of Kolkata’s Smart City initiative, New Town boasts proximity to essential amenities like healthcare facilities, educational institutions, IT hubs, and entertainment centers.

The area is well-connected through major roads such as Biswa Bangla Sarani and the New Town Expressway, ensuring smooth connectivity to key areas like Salt Lake, Sector V, and Rajarhat. Public transport, including buses, auto-rickshaws, and ride-hailing services, enhances mobility within the city. The upcoming New Garia-Airport Metro line is expected to further boost connectivity for Purti Veda residents, offering direct access to places like Dum Dum, Tollygunge, and New Garia. Moreover, the proximity to Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport adds convenience for frequent travelers.

Spacious Living and Thoughtful Design

Purti Veda spans across 2 acres and is designed to provide a premium residential experience. It offers a variety of 2BHK and 3BHK apartments to meet the needs of modern homebuyers. Apartment sizes range from 895 to 1128 sq.ft., ensuring ample space for a comfortable lifestyle. With 4 residential towers of G+12 floors and a total of 184 exclusive residences, Purti Veda is crafted to maximize natural light and ventilation. Whether you prefer a cozy 2BHK or a spacious 3BHK, each unit is thoughtfully designed to provide residents with a sense of openness and tranquility.
