Elita Garden Vista - Project Overview

Experience Luxurious Living at Elita Garden Vista in New Town

Elita Garden Vista is a meticulously crafted residential project located in the vibrant heart of New Town, Action Area III. This luxurious development is a collaborative effort from renowned real estate developers JB Group, Sureka Group, and Merlin Group, who have combined their expertise to create an exceptional living space that caters to the diverse needs and aspirations of modern homeowners.

Prime Location in Action Area III, New Town

Action Area III in New Town, Kolkata, epitomizes contemporary urban living. Renowned for its strategic positioning, this area offers seamless connectivity to major transport networks, making it an ideal choice for residents seeking convenience and accessibility. Key roadways, including the Major Arterial Road and the Southern Bypass, provide easy access to the bustling city center and surrounding regions. Additionally, the proximity to upcoming metro stations enhances the commuting experience, ensuring that residents of Elita Garden Vista can effortlessly reach essential destinations such as business districts, educational institutions, and healthcare facilities.

The prime location of Elita Garden Vista allows residents to enjoy the best of both worlds: a vibrant urban lifestyle combined with the tranquility of lush green surroundings. This community-oriented environment features essential amenities, including shopping malls, recreational hubs, and parks, contributing to a high quality of life for families and professionals alike.

Spacious and Thoughtfully Designed Residences

Spread over an impressive 25 acres, Elita Garden Vista offers a variety of residential units designed for spacious living. The project features 1,511 units available in different sizes, including 2BHK and 3BHK apartments, as well as larger configurations. Each thoughtfully designed home accommodates modern lifestyles with floor plans that maximize space and functionality. The residential units boast a super built-up area ranging from approximately 999 to 1,742 sq. ft., allowing for comfortable living and flexibility in interior design.

The architectural design of Elita Garden Vista is a celebration of aesthetics and functionality. Sleek, modern towers rise majestically against the Kolkata skyline, showcasing a harmonious blend of glass, steel, and greenery. Expansive windows provide breathtaking views of the city while inviting ample natural light, creating a warm and inviting ambiance within each residence.

Investment Potential

Conclusion

In conclusion, Elita Garden Vista emerges as an ideal residential destination, promising an unparalleled living experience amidst lush greenery and contemporary comforts.
