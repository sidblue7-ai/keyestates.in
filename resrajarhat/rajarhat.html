Rajarhat

Rajarhat, a prominent neighborhood under the jurisdiction of the Bidhan Nagar Municipal Corporation, is located on the northern outskirts of Kolkata. It is surrounded by key areas such as New Town, Salt Lake, Baguiati, and Lake Town.

The area boasts strong infrastructure with reputed schools, hospitals, malls, and IT hubs. It offers a mix of ready-to-move and under-construction residential developments.

Rajarhat is highly preferred by professionals due to proximity to IT parks and excellent connectivity, making it one of Kolkata’s fastest-growing real estate hubs.
